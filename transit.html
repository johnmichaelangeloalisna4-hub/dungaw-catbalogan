Vehicle Guide

Modes of Transport in Catbalogan

Understanding local transit options makes getting around effortless and budget-friendly. Here is how each vehicle functions in the city.

Motorized Tricycle

₱12 – ₱15 / special ₱50–₱100

The king of downtown streets! These customized passenger sidecars carry up to 4–5 passengers and run 24/7 across every street in the city center.

Best For: Downtown hotels, City Hall, Provincial Capitol, Pier 1, and the Public Market.

Habal-habal (Motorcycle)

₱150 – ₱200 / roundtrip

Single motorbikes driven by experienced local riders capable of taking steep mountain trails and countryside roads where four-wheel vehicles cannot go.

Best For: Bangon Falls, Mount Naga, upland farming communities, and off-grid eco-adventures.

Motorized Outrigger Banca

₱50 – ₱80 / charter ₱500–₱800

Traditional twin-outrigger wooden motorized boats docked at Pier 1 and Barangay Guinsorongan coastlines, serving island barangays across Maqueda Bay.

Best For: Darahuway Guti Island marine sanctuary and coastal island-hopping day tours.

Multicab & Jeepney

₱15 – ₱30 per passenger

High-capacity shared public utility vehicles that run scheduled routes north and south along the Pan-Philippine (Maharlika) Highway corridor.

Best For: Buri Island causeway, Silanga, Jiabong (Tahong Capital), and northern outskirts.

Helpful Commuter Phrases (Waray-Waray • English)

Catbalogan drivers are warm and friendly! Use these easy phrases when riding around the city:

"Pira it plete ngadto ha...?"
Meaning: "How much is the fare to...?"
Example: "Pira it plete ngadto ha Buri Island?"
"Lugar la po! / Para!"
Meaning: "Please pull over! / I am getting off here!"
Say when you reach your destination to stop the vehicle.
"Special trip po ini?"
Meaning: "Can we hire this as a private charter trip?"
Useful for direct non-stop rides with luggage.
"Damo nga salamat!"
Meaning: "Thank you very much!"
Polite greeting upon alighting.
Official Transport Hubs

Vehicle Terminals & Transport Stations

Locate the nearest passenger terminal, van dispatch, boat wharf, or tricycle stand in Catbalogan City. Easily track vehicles to your next destination.

Catbalogan Integrated Bus Terminal

Provincial Bus Hub
Location: Diversion Road / North Coastal Highway, Brgy. Muñoz
Vehicles: Aircon & Regular Provincial Buses (Philtranco, DLTBCo, Silver Star, Ultra Bus)
Destinations: Metro Manila, Tacloban City, Calbayog City, Ormoc, Southern Leyte
Operating 24/7 with daily scheduled dispatch

Grand Tours Van Terminal

Regional UV Express Terminal
Location: San Francisco St. corner Del Rosario, City Proper
Vehicles: Aircon UV Express Passenger Vans
Destinations: Tacloban (approx. 2 hrs), Calbayog (1.5 hrs), Borongan (2.5 hrs), Guiuan, Catarman
Departs every 30-45 minutes (5:00 AM – 7:00 PM)

Duptours Shuttle Terminal

Inter-City Van Express
Location: Mabini Avenue corner Allen Avenue, Downtown
Vehicles: Aircon Passenger Van Shuttles
Destinations: Direct non-stop shuttle service to Tacloban City & Calbayog City
Hourly departures (5:30 AM – 6:30 PM)

Catbalogan Port / Pier 1

Passenger Ferry Seaport
Location: Curry Avenue Seafront, Port Area
Vehicles: Commercial Passenger & RORO Ferries (Roble Shipping, Cokaliong)
Destinations: Cebu City Pier (overnight passenger voyages) & Island Ports
Nightly passenger ferry departures (7:00 PM – 8:00 PM)

Catbalogan Motorboat & Pumpboat Wharf

Island Banca Pier
Location: Lower Curry Avenue Seafront (near Port Area)
Vehicles: Motorized Outrigger Bancas (Pumpboats)
Destinations: Sierra Island (La Sierra Resort), Darahuway Guti, Buri Island, Daram
Regular fare: PHP 30 - 80 • Private charter available

Central Downtown Tricycle Terminal

City Tricycle Stand
Location: Rizal Avenue corner San Bartolome St. (City Plaza / Imelda Park)
Vehicles: Motorized Tricycles (TODA Licensed)
Destinations: Downtown hotels, accredited restaurants, pier, public market, hospitals
Regulated standard fare: PHP 12.00 – 15.00 within city proper

Northbound Tricycle Terminal (Silanga / Buri)

North Coastal Tricycle Stand
Location: San Roque Street (North Highway Exit)
Vehicles: Tricycles & Habal-habal Motorcycles
Destinations: Buri Island Boardwalk & Airport, Silanga, Guinsorongan coastal spots
Special fare to Buri: PHP 25.00 – 40.00

Southbound Multicab & Jeepney Stand

South Coastal Multicab Stand
Location: Taft Avenue corner San Bartolome St.
Vehicles: Public Multicabs & Regular Jeepneys
Destinations: Payao Mangrove Reserve, Brgy. Mercedes, Jiabong boundary
Regular fare: PHP 15.00 – 25.00
